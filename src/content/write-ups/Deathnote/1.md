My first step was navigating to see the webpage on port 80. It immediately redirected me to an unreachable wordpress site, so not much I could do there myself.

Thankfully after a little poking, nikto found me a whole bunch of hidden wordpress directories, the bullseye being `/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/`

This directory had `user.txt`  and `notes.txt`, both obviously formatted as a username wordlist, and a password wordlist respectively. 

It didn't take long for Hydra to find a match, and just like that I was into the account `l`. 

The home directory for this account had a text file that I quickly recognised as being encrypted into Brainfuck, which I encountered in an earlier box. This however just translated into a quote or reference to the show, that unfortunately, I don't get (I should probably watch it). `i think u got the shell , but you wont be able to kill me -kira`

I had a look around, found another account called kira, but no way to get inside at the moment.

I started to fumble around blindly, until I ended up finding the L directory hidden in `/opt`

This had a small decryption task, which even directly told you to use cyberchef. This did not take long to solve, as the text was recognisably hexadecimal, which turned into base64, that turned into plaintext!

The text contained a password, which surprise surprise, ended up being for the other account!

Once in, I found a file containing more base64, which decrypted to: 
> please protect one of the following 
> 1. L (/opt)
> 2. Misa (/var)

/var lead to another show reference I didn't get, but then I realised, I hadn't even checked if kira had sudo privilege!

Lo and behold, the account did! I simply typed `sudo /bin/bash`, and just like that I was root!

In `/root`, I found the flag, and completed this box!
